# Vocabulary Mapping v2.1

## Rule
Story text may use natural inflected forms. Highlight the surface form, but map vocabulary cards and learning identity back to the **lemma**.

| Lemma | Forms | Level | 中文核心義 | Story example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **reveal** | reveal, reveals, revealed, revealing | L3 | 揭露；顯示原本不知道的事 | The message did not reveal the truth, but it gave her something real to follow. |
| **specific** | specific | L3 | 特定的；明確的 | Was it an accident, or had someone left it there for a specific reason? |
| **prevent** | prevent, prevents, prevented, preventing | L3 | 阻止；防止 | This should prevent anyone from finding out too early. |
| **determine** | determine, determines, determined, determining | L3 | 確定；判定 | Although entering alone made her nervous, she wanted to determine why everyone seemed afraid of this room. |
| **observe** | observe, observes, observed, observing | L3 | 觀察；注意看 | Mia stopped behind a wall and observed him carefully. |
| **anxiety** | anxiety | L3 | 焦慮；不安 | The answer did nothing to reduce Mia's anxiety. |
| **identify** | identify, identifies, identified, identifying | L4 | 辨認；確認身分或特徵 | Mia tried to identify the person, but the hood covered most of the stranger's face. |
| **indication** | indication, indications | L4 | 跡象；指示 | Maybe it was another indication that this was more than a joke. |
| **observation** | observation, observations | L4 | 觀察；觀察所得 | That simple observation made her curious. |
| **mysterious** | mysterious | L4 | 神祕的；難以解釋的 | Whatever was happening at school was becoming more mysterious by the minute. |

## Popup behavior
- Display lemma as the headword.
- If the story surface form is different (e.g. `observed`), the clicked text remains highlighted but the card title is `observe`.
- Show level, Chinese core meaning, the frozen story example, and the short explanation from the CSV.
- Do not invent extra meanings during implementation.
