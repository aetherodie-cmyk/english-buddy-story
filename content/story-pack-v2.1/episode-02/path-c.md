# Episode 02C — Tell Emma

Mia decided not to follow Leo and not to enter Room 403 alone.

Instead, she called Emma, her best friend.

Ten minutes later, Emma returned to school. Mia showed her the messages.

Emma's reaction surprised her.

“I got one too,” Emma said.

She showed Mia a message from that morning:

“If anything unusual happens today, stay away from Room 403.”

Mia felt a sudden wave of **anxiety**.

They compared the messages and **observed** several differences. Mia's messages mentioned Leo, while Emma's warned her to stay away.

“That can't be random,” Mia said.

Emma nodded. “Maybe they were sent for **specific** reasons.”

Another message on Emma's phone read:

**17:30 — Science Building — Locker 26**

A small triangle appeared beside the number.

Neither girl could **identify** what the symbol meant.

“Maybe it's an **indication** of where to look,” Mia said.

“Or a warning,” Emma replied.

They needed more information to **determine** which idea made sense.

Emma wanted to tell a teacher, but Mia wasn't sure. What if the messages were meant to **prevent** them from trusting the wrong person?

Then Emma pointed toward the fourth floor.

Someone was standing at the window of Room 403.

The figure disappeared a second later.

“That was **mysterious**,” Emma whispered.

Mia stared at the empty window.

The messages still did not **reveal** who was behind them.

But now both girls knew one thing:

At 17:30, something connected to Locker 26 was going to happen.
