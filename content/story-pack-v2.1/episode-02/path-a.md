# Episode 02A — Follow Leo

Mia hesitated for only a second. Then she followed Leo.

He left the school through a side door and crossed the playground. Mia stayed far enough behind him to avoid being seen.

Near the old science building, Leo slowed down. Mia stopped behind a wall and **observed** him carefully.

Another person was waiting under the roof.

“Did anything happen in Room 403 today?” the stranger asked.

Leo shook his head. “Not yet.”

The answer did nothing to reduce Mia's **anxiety**.

The stranger handed Leo a small envelope.

“This should **prevent** anyone from finding out too early.”

“Finding out what?” Leo asked.

The stranger did not answer.

Mia tried to **identify** the person, but the hood covered most of the stranger's face. The only clear detail she could see was a silver key hanging from the person's bag.

Then her phone buzzed.

“Look at the ground.”

When Mia looked up again, the stranger was already walking away.

Leo left a moment later.

Mia waited, then stepped out from behind the wall. A small piece of paper lay near the place where they had been standing.

Was it an accident, or had someone left it there for a **specific** reason?

She picked it up.

**17:30 — Science Building — Locker 26**

Mia read it twice.

The message did not **reveal** the truth, but it gave her something real to follow.

Then she noticed a small mark beside the number 26.

It looked like a triangle.

Maybe it was another **indication** that this was more than a joke.

Whatever was happening at school was becoming more **mysterious** by the minute.
