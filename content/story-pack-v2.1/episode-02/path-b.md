# Episode 02B — Room 403

Mia watched Leo disappear down the stairs. Then she turned toward Room 403.

Although entering alone made her nervous, she wanted to **determine** why everyone seemed afraid of this room.

She pushed the door.

It opened.

At first, the classroom looked normal: old desks, a whiteboard, several boxes and a broken computer.

Mia stood still and **observed** the room.

One desk was much cleaner than the others.

That simple **observation** made her curious.

Inside the desk was a small envelope.

Mia hesitated, then opened it.

There was a photograph of the science building. On the back, someone had written:

**17:30 — Locker 26**

There was also a small triangle beside the number.

Mia tried to **identify** the place shown in the photograph. One window was circled in red.

Why that **specific** window?

Suddenly, footsteps approached Room 403.

Mia moved behind the door.

Her phone buzzed.

“Don't let them see the photograph.”

The message increased her **anxiety**. Whoever was texting her seemed to know exactly what she was doing.

Was the sender trying to **prevent** someone from discovering the photograph?

Or was the photograph meant to **reveal** something?

The door began to open.

“Is anyone in there?”

Mia recognized the voice.

It wasn't Leo.

It was the principal.

That was the clearest **indication** yet that Room 403 mattered.
