# Episode 01 — The Wrong Message

## Scene 01 — The Message
It was almost five o'clock when Mia finally left the school library. Most students had already gone home, and the rain made the hallway feel strangely empty.

As Mia walked toward the stairs, her phone suddenly buzzed.

“Don't tell anyone what happened in Room 403.”

Mia stopped. She didn't recognize the number. She was about to ignore it when another message appeared:

“You know what I mean.”

Now she began to hesitate.

## Scene 02 — A Suspicious Encounter
Room 403 was an old classroom on the fourth floor. Students rarely used it, but Mia remembered seeing two teachers there that afternoon.

She tried to convince herself that it was just a joke. Then she heard footsteps upstairs.

Leo, a student from her class, appeared at the end of the hallway.

“Why are you still here?” he asked.

Leo looked back toward the stairs. His behavior seemed suspicious. Mia began to suspect that he knew something about Room 403.

But she had no evidence.

## Scene 03 — He Knows the Truth
“You should go home,” Leo said quietly.

“Why?”

Leo looked at her for a few seconds. “Just trust me.”

Before Mia could ask another question, he walked away.

Her phone buzzed again.

“He knows the truth.”

Was Leo trying to protect her, or had he sent the messages? Following him could be a risk, but there was now one clear clue: Room 403.

Maybe she should investigate it herself.

## Scene 04 — The Mystery Continues
Then Mia remembered something.

Tomorrow morning, the principal was going to inspect every classroom on the fourth floor.

Why?

Mia looked toward the stairs.

For the first time, this no longer felt like a joke. It felt like a mystery.

And somehow, she had become part of it.
