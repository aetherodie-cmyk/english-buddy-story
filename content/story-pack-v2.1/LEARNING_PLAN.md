## Grammar Discovery
`Although A, B.`

Example:
> Although entering alone made her nervous, she wanted to determine why everyone seemed afraid of this room.

## Writing Skill
**Show, don't just tell.**

Flat: `Mia was nervous.`  
Story-like: `The answer did nothing to reduce Mia's anxiety.` / `Mia felt a sudden wave of anxiety.`
